# Creating the Block Diagram Content

Defining the content of a software component includes the following steps:

1. [Placing a Signature Element](PlaceElement.md)
1. Creating the necessary elements:
1. [creating operators](PositionOperator.md), among them [If statements](UseIf.md), [switch](UseSwitch.md) and the [while loop](Usewhileloop.md)
1. [connecting diagram elements](Connectdiagram.md)
1. [editing the sequence calls](BDE_WorkingOnSequenceCalls.md)
1. add implementation casts to [connections](Addtoconnect.md) or [operators](Addimplementation.md)
1. add [a comment](Addcomment.md) or [a literal](BDE_AddLiteral.md)
1. use [assert](BDE_Use_AssertOperator.md) or [conversion](BDE_UseConversionOperator.md) operators
1. move or copy diagram items [in the same diagram](BDE_Cutcopypaste.md) or [between diagrams](BDE_CopyMove_Items_betweenDiagram.md)
1. [rename or delete diagram items](BDE_rename_delete.md)
1. [search and delete unused elements](BDE_SearchDeleteUnusedElements.md)
1. [use graphical hierarchies and statement blocks](BDE_UseHierarchiesStatementBlocks.md)
