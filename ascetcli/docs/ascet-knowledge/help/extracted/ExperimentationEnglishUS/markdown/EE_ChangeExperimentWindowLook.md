# Changing the Experiment Window Look

To change the look of the experiment window, proceed as follows.

1. Click the ![](button_switch.gif) Expand/Collapse Window button to hide the component display of the experiment window.
1. Click on the ![](button_foreground.gif) Always on top button, and the Physical Experiment window will always remain in the foreground.
1. Click the ![](button_switch.gif) Expand/Collapse Window button again to make the component visible again.
