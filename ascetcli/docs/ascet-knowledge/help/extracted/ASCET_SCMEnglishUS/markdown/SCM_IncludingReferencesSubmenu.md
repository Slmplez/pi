To handle entire groups of ASCET items in Subversion, some of the commands in the [SCM](SCM_ASCET-SCM_Menu.md) menu or the Source Control context menu are also available for selected items together with all of their references. They can be found in the Including References submenu.

- Update with References

Performs an [Update](SCM_ASCET-SCM_Menu.md#Update) for the selected item and for its references.

- Update to Latest Revision with References

Performs an [Update to Latest Revision](SCM_ASCET-SCM_Menu.md#Update_to_Latest_Revision) for the selected item and for its references.

- Update and Lock with References

Performs an [Update](SCM_ASCET-SCM_Menu.md#Update) and [Lock](SCM_ASCET-SCM_Menu.md#Get_Lock) for the selected item and for its references.

- Commit with References

Performs a [Commit](SCM_ASCET-SCM_Menu.md#Commit) for the selected item and for its references.

- Add and Commit with References

Performs an [Add and Commit](SCM_ASCET-SCM_Menu.md#Add_and_Commit) for the selected item and for its references.
