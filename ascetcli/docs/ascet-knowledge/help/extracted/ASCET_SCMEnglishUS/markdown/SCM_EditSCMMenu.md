1. Enter <MenuItem id="<command ID>"/> to define a menu item.
1. Enter <MenuItem /> to define a separator.
1. Enter a section <MenuItem label="<submenu name>"> ... </MenuItem> to define a submenu.
1. To define the submenu entries, place <MenuItem id="<command ID>"/> and <MenuItem /> lines between <MenuItem label="<submenu name>"> and </MenuItem>.

See also [Example 2](SCM_CustomizeMenusIcons_Examples.md#Example2) and [Example 3](SCM_CustomizeMenusIcons_Examples.md#Example3) in [Customizing Menus and Icons - Examples](SCM_CustomizeMenusIcons_Examples.md).
