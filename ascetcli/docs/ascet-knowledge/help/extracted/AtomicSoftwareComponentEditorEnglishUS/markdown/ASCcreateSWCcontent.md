# Creating the SWC Content

Defining the content of a software component includes the following steps:

1. [Placing a Signature Element](ascplaceelement.md)
1. Creating the necessary elements:
1. [creating operators](ascpositionoperator.md), among them [If statements](ascuseif.md), [switch](ascuseswitch.md) and the [while loop](ascusewhileloop.md)
1. [connecting diagram elements](ascconnectdiagram.md)
1. [edit the sequence calls](ASCeditSequenceCalls.md)
1. add implementation casts to [connections](ascaddimplcasttoconnect.md) or [operators](ascaddimplementationcast.md)
1. add [a comment](BlockDiagramEditorEnglishUS.chm::/Addcomment.htm) or [a literal](BlockDiagramEditorEnglishUS.chm::/BDE_AddLiteral.htm)
1. move or copy diagram items [in the same diagram](asccutcopypaste.md) or [between diagrams](asc_copymove_items_betweendiagram.md)
1. [rename or delete diagram items](ascrenamedeleteElement.md)
1. [search and delete unused elements](ASC_SearchDeleteUnusedElements.md)
1. [use graphical hierarchies and statement blocks](ASC_UseGraphicalHierarchies_StatementBlocks.md)
