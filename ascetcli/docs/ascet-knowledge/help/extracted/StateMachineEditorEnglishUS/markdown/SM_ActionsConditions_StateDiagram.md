# Editing Actions/Conditions in the State Diagram

You have to perform the following steps to specify an action or condition in the state diagram.

1. [Specify entry/static/exit actions in the state editor.](specifying_actions.md)
1. [Specify conditions and transition actions in the transition editor.](specifying_conditions_transition.md)
1. You can [cut, copy and paste ESDL code](cut_copyand%20paste.md).
1. You can [insert comment lines](automatic_insertion.md)
1. [Activate/deactivating outlining of actions/conditions](SM_activate_deactivate_outlining_of_actions_conditions_.md) according to your needs.
